## Project Overview
This project explores the chemical properties of red and white wines through data analysis and interactive visualizations. Using Jupyter Notebook, Python, Pandas, and Altair, I investigate relationships between wine characteristics such as alcohol content, density, chlorides, sulfur dioxide levels, and quality ratings.

The goal is to explore patterns in the data, compare red and white wine characteristics, and demonstrate how data visualization can help uncover relationships within a dataset.

## Objectives
•	Explore the relationship between physiochemical properties in red & white wine.   
•	Compare the distribution of wine quality ratings across red and white wines.  
•	Examine differences in chloride concentrations between wine types.  
•	Visualize the relationship between free and total sulfur dioxide levels.  
•	Practice exploratory data analysis (EDA) and communicate findings through data visualization.

## Tools & Tech
•	Python — Data analysis and manipulation        
•	Pandas — Data cleaning, filtering, and combining datasets    
•	Altair — Statistical and interactive visualizations  
•	Jupyter Notebook — Exploratory analysis and documentation

## Dataset
The project uses two datasets containing physicochemical measurements and quality ratings for red and white wines.
Each dataset includes variables such as:

| Variable | Description|
| --- | --- |
| Alcohol	| Alcohol content by volume|
| Density| Density of the wine | 
Chlorides| Chloride Concentration 

The datasets are loaded separately and combined where needed to facilitate comparisons between red and white wines.

## Exploratory Analysis & Visualizations
1. Alcohol Content vs. Density
Scatter plots compare alcohol content and density for red and white wines, with color representing the quality rating.  
**Analytical focus**:  
•	Examine the relationship between alcohol content and density.  
•	Explore how quality ratings are distributed across the two variables.  
•	Compare patterns between red and white wines.
2. Alcohol Content vs. Wine Quality
Faceted heatmaps display the frequency of observations across alcohol-content ranges and quality ratings for both wine types.  
**Analytical focus**:  
•	Compare the distribution of quality ratings.   
•	Explore how alcohol content varies across quality categories.  
•	Identify areas with higher concentrations of observations.   
3. Chloride Concentration Comparison
Box plots compare chloride distributions in red and white wines.   
**Analytical focus**:  
•	Compare the central tendency and spread of chloride concentrations.  
•	Examine differences in variability between wine types.  
•	Identify potential outliers in the distributions.
4. Free vs. Total Sulfur Dioxide
Heatmaps visualize the concentration of observations across free and total sulfur dioxide levels, separately for red and white wines.  
**Analytical focus**:  
•	Explore the relationship between free and total sulfur dioxide.  
•	Compare concentration patterns between wine types.  
•	Examine how sulfur dioxide measurements are distributed across observations.  

The sulfur dioxide visualizations filter observations to total sulfur dioxide values of 300 or less and free sulfur dioxide values of 100 or less.
